Monstrous Feast is leaving Early Access. The module is on public GitHub now as **1.0.0**.

Butcher the monsters you kill, cook at camp, and feed the party.

harvest creatures after a fight, add recopies to the Cookbook, eat meals with various buffs for the party! (Runs on its own, or hooks into Respite when both are installed)

Iv'e included/kept to pantry items, so you still have bread, flour, oils, eggs as staples to cook fuller meals with. And seasoning to give things a boost, yo can't just travel without your basic ingredients and hope to cook up a monster feast without the basics - Senshi would be proud.

If you already had the EA build, update to **1.0.0**. Fresh install is just Foundry > **Install Module** > this manifest URL:

[https://github.com/ionrift-gm/ionrift-monstrous-feast/releases/latest/download/module.json](https://github.com/ionrift-gm/ionrift-monstrous-feast/releases/latest/download/module.json)

(Needs Ionrift Library 2.5.12+.)

  
As always if something weird shows up in the butcher or cook loop, drop it in Discord.

Oh and if you make Homebrew recipes please add them in **#community-packs**!
