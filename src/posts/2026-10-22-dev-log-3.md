Sunday brought seven free module updates. Quartermaster can turn chests already on the map into loot caches, and Respite hides the rest steps that don't apply. Tokens wading through Waterline water get their own sounds when Resonance is running too. Add-on Modules picks up the Foundry-listed updates, and Monstrous Feast, which is free, still installs from its GitHub release.

Happy adventuring.
