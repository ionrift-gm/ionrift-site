Sunday was a big release day, with seven free modules updated at once. Quartermaster can now turn the chests already in your world into loot caches, and Respite skips the rest steps that don't apply to your table. Tokens also make a splash wading through Waterline water if you run Resonance alongside it. Foundry's update check in Add-on Modules will pick them all up.

Happy adventuring.
