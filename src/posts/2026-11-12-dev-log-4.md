Dev log #4 rounds up the Ionrift releases for Foundry VTT from late October and early November. Ionrift Voice 1.0 takes proximity voice chat out of Early Access, with walls, doors and floors shaping what players hear and a Discord voice bridge, and Quartermaster 1.13 now runs on any Foundry game system, with loot caches and World Items loot for systems like Old-School Essentials. Respite, Waterline, Resonance, Library and Quartermaster also picked up translation support and fixes for flying tokens over water.

Happy adventuring.
